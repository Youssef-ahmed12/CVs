Introduction

About Dr. Amani

Dr. Amani Abdel Fattah Bayoumi Hafez is a Consultant and academic staff member in the Department of Forensic Medicine & Clinical Toxicology at Kasr Al-Aini Hospital, Faculty of Medicine, Cairo University. Graduating from the Faculty of Medicine, Cairo University in 2001 — after her secondary education at the prestigious French school Mère de Dieu — she built a career that spans clinical consultation, postgraduate supervision, advanced research, and tireless community advocacy.


Her Master's thesis focused on domestic violence, and her Doctoral research examined the toxic effects of Gibberellic acid in an experimental model — reflecting a career that bridges laboratory science with urgent social realities. She has supervised numerous Master's and M.D. theses, delivered hundreds of public lectures across Egyptian universities, and represented Egypt at major international scientific forums.


As Coordinator of the Anti-Harassment Unit at Kasr Al-Aini Hospital for five successive years and a long-time champion of the "Support Yourself by Yourself" initiative, Dr. Amani is widely celebrated for her commitment to empowering women and protecting the most vulnerable members of society.
